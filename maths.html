Learners who want to refresh mathematical foundations before or alongside analytics and introductory machine learning.

Example learning outline

01

Working with quantities

Percentages, ratios, rates, units, and the habits that prevent common calculation errors.

02

Descriptive statistics

Distributions, measures of center and spread, outliers, and clear summaries of a dataset.

03

Probability and inference

Basic probability, sampling, uncertainty, and how to interpret statistical evidence carefully.

04

Maths behind models

Build intuition for vectors, functions, and optimization concepts used in introductory ML.
