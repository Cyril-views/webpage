Privacy notice

This plain-language notice is for Y-Hat Analytics, an online education and technology training institute. Before accepting enrolments, the institute must confirm and publish its legal entity name, postal address, privacy contact, service providers, data regions, and retention schedule. This notice should be reviewed for the laws that apply to the institute and its learners.

Last updated: 9 November 2026. Notice version: privacy-notice-v4.

Who we are and how to contact us

Y-Hat Analytics is the operating name currently used for online technology education and training. The legal entity responsible for personal information and its postal address must be confirmed before enrolment opens. For privacy questions or requests, email yhatanalytics03@gmail.com or call 7094959197. A designated grievance contact, if required by applicable law, must be published here before launch.

Information we collect

The information collected depends on how you use our services. A course enquiry may include your name, email, phone number, selected course or bundle, preferred class time, experience level, learning goals, and a record of your consent. If you enrol, we may also need information to create and administer your learner account, deliver classes, record attendance, assess coursework, provide support, and issue completion records. We will explain any additional required information when a feature is introduced.

If you pay for a course, the payment provider processes the payment. We may receive transaction references, payment status, amount, billing details, and refund information. We do not ask you to send card numbers, security codes, passwords, government ID numbers, or sensitive personal information in an enquiry form, email, or chat.

Our website and hosting providers may generate technical information such as IP address, device/browser details, request time, and security logs. We do not currently use advertising pixels or behavioural advertising cookies. If analytics or other tracking tools are added, this notice and any applicable consent choices will be updated first.

Why we use information

  • Respond to enquiries and provide course, schedule, eligibility, and fee information.
  • Register learners and deliver online classes, learning materials, assessments, support, and completion records.
  • Process and reconcile payments, cancellations, refunds, and required financial records through a payment provider.
  • Maintain the safety, security, reliability, and integrity of our services, prevent misuse, and resolve disputes.
  • Meet legal obligations and establish or defend legal claims.
  • Send course news or marketing only where you have separately chosen to receive it; you may opt out at any time.

We use information only for purposes that are explained to you and permitted by applicable law. Where consent is the basis for processing, you may withdraw it by contacting us; this does not affect processing already carried out or records we must retain by law.

Online learning and community features

Online classes, learning platforms, video meetings, messaging groups, and learner communities may be provided by third-party services. If we introduce these features, we will identify the services and the information shared with them before asking you to join. Community posts and profile details may be visible to other members. Please do not share another learner's contact details, work, or personal information without permission.

When information is shared

We may share the minimum information needed with authorised institute personnel and service providers that support hosting, databases, email, online classes, learning management, customer support, security, and payment processing. Providers must be selected and configured appropriately for their role. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect people or services, or in connection with a lawful business reorganisation.

We do not sell learners' personal information. We do not share enquiry details for unrelated third-party marketing. The institute must publish the actual provider names and relevant data locations before collecting learner information through a new or migrated service.

Where information is processed

The previous application used Google Cloud Run and Firestore in the asia-south1 region. The selected replacement is Cloudflare Workers with D1, but it has not yet been provisioned or deployed and is not currently used for learner records. Before collecting information on the replacement, the institute must verify the active provider configuration, data processing locations, and applicable provider terms, then update this notice to match. Provider terms may permit limited processing in other locations.

How long we keep information

Unconverted course enquiries have a retention target of 12 months after submission, after which they should be deleted. The Cloudflare D1 schema records this expiry date, but automatic deletion has not been configured or verified. The previous Firestore deployment has not been verified to automatically delete expired enquiries. Learner, course-delivery, community, payment, and backup records require separate approved retention periods; no online payment is currently accepted.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, withdraw consent, object to or restrict certain processing, or raise a complaint with a relevant authority. To make a request, contact us using the details above. We may need to verify your identity before acting and may retain information where the law requires or permits us to do so.

You may opt out of non-essential marketing at any time using the unsubscribe option in a message or by contacting us. This will not prevent service-related messages about a course or payment you requested.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information and services, including access controls and server-side validation. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to learner information should be limited to authorised people who need it for their work. If a data incident occurs, we will take the steps required by applicable law.

Children

Our current courses are intended for adults. Do not submit personal information for anyone under 18. We will not knowingly enrol a minor unless we first establish and publish an appropriate guardian-consent process and comply with applicable law.

Third-party websites

Our website may link to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices, not this one. Review those notices before sharing information with them.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the institute adds courses, learner accounts, community features, payment services, or technology providers. The latest version and update date will be posted on this page. Material changes will be brought to learners' attention where required.

Required legal and business approvals before live launch

Before we accept real student applications or online payments, the institution must complete the following:

  • Confirm the registered legal entity name, business registration status, and business address.
  • Publish a privacy contact and grievance/contact escalation path for students.
  • Approve the final refund, cancellation, fee schedule, and payment provider policy.
  • Finalise the retention period for enquiries, learner records, fees, and backup data.
  • Review all vendors and data processors, including hosting, email, video, LMS, and payment providers.
  • Approve the student offer terms, class schedule, delivery format, and age/guardian rules.
  • Confirm the governing law, dispute process, and complaint handling path for India-based services.

These requirements are part of the institution's prelaunch legal and business gate. Until they are confirmed and approved, this site remains in a demo/prelaunch state and does not accept live enrolment or payment.
