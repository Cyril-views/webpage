Cloud data engineering

GCP Data Engineering Professional Program

Build practical skills across SQL, Cloud Storage, BigQuery, Pub/Sub, Dataflow, Composer, IAM, monitoring, cost optimization, and end-to-end projects.

14-16 WEEK PROGRAM60+ LIVE HOURS TARGETFOUNDING COHORT PRICING

Who this path can suit

Learners with SQL foundations who want to design and explain production-shaped data pipelines on Google Cloud.

  • Build a warehouse and lake storage foundation
  • Understand batch, streaming, and orchestration patterns
  • Reason about IAM, monitoring, reliability, and cost

Example learning outline

01

SQL, GCS & BigQuery

Model warehouse tables, load files to Cloud Storage, query in BigQuery, and apply partitioning, clustering, and cost-aware SQL.

02

Pub/Sub and Dataflow

Compare batch and streaming designs, understand messaging concepts, and build a guided data-processing pipeline.

03

Transformation and orchestration

Explore Dataform or dbt patterns alongside Airflow/Composer scheduling, dependencies, retries, and data-quality checks.

04

Operate a secure platform

Apply IAM concepts and review monitoring, reliability, access, and cost-optimization practices.

05

End-to-end capstone

Plan, build, validate, and present a data workflow with documented architecture and operational tradeoffs.
