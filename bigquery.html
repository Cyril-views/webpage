BigQuery

BigQuery is Google Cloud’s serverless data warehouse used for analysing large datasets quickly and efficiently.

What BigQuery helps with

  • Running fast SQL queries over large data volumes
  • Organising data into tables and datasets
  • Supporting dashboards and reporting workflows
  • Working with structured and semi-structured data

Why it is useful

  • Popular for analytics and business intelligence
  • Scales well for growing data needs
  • Integrates smoothly with other Google Cloud services
  • Useful for learners moving into data engineering and analytics roles
