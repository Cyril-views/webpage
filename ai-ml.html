Topics can be sequenced to match learner experience, from AI literacy and data preparation through hands-on model development and GenAI application patterns.

Example learning outline

01

Python, statistics & data foundations

Prepare data, revisit probability and statistics, and frame measurable machine-learning problems.

Classical machine learning

Train regression, classification, and unsupervised models with sound validation practices.

04

Deep learning, NLP & embeddings

Develop neural-network intuition and explore language-processing patterns used in modern AI systems.

05

LLMs, RAG & agentic workflows

Explore prompts, retrieval-augmented generation, evaluation, tool use, and responsible agent design.

06

Deployment & capstone

Scope, evaluate, and present an applied AI project with safety, privacy, and human-review considerations.
