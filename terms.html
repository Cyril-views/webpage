Enrollment requests and offers

Submitting the website form is an enquiry only; it does not create an enrollment contract or reserve a seat. Y-Hat Analytics must send a written offer stating the confirmed program, cohort dates, schedule, delivery format, prerequisites, tuition, applicable taxes, payment deadline, and refund/cancellation terms. Enrollment begins only after the learner accepts that offer and completes its stated requirements.

Course information

Program outlines describe the intended learning topics. The website displays Founding Cohort and regular prices, target live hours, durations, and bundle prices for planning; these do not create an enrollment contract or reserve a seat. Cohort start dates remain to be announced. The intended learning model is 2-3 live online classes each week, approximately 1.5-2 hours per class, supported by assignments, guided labs, recordings, and learner support. The written offer controls if it differs from website content.

Delivery and participation

The intended format is live online instruction with guided practice. Exact dates, holiday changes, required equipment/software, attendance expectations, recording availability, and support hours must be stated in the written offer. Learners are responsible for maintaining a suitable internet connection and protecting their account credentials if an account service is introduced.

Fees, cancellations, and refunds

No payment is collected through the current enrollment-request form. No refund or cancellation commitment is made by this draft. Before taking payment, the academy must publish and provide a legally reviewed policy covering learner cancellation, academy cancellation or rescheduling, refunds, transfers, payment failures, taxes, and any payment-provider charges.

Learning materials and conduct

Course materials should be used for the enrolled learner's personal learning and must not be redistributed, resold, or used to infringe another person's rights. Learners are expected to communicate respectfully and avoid sharing other learners' personal information. Final content-license and conduct terms require business approval.

Career support and outcomes

Career preparation may include project presentation, CV feedback, or interview practice when stated in the written offer. Training and career guidance do not guarantee a job, interview, placement, salary, or specific outcome.

Applicable law and contact

This draft is intended for an India-based service. The registered entity, governing-law clause, dispute process, and competent court location must be reviewed and completed by Indian counsel before publication. Questions may be sent to yhatanalytics03@gmail.com or 7094959197.

Business and legal launch prerequisites

Before the institution opens live enrolment or accepts payment, the following must be finalised and approved:

  • The registered legal entity name, office address, and business registration details.
  • The final student agreement, refund policy, and course fee schedule.
  • The privacy notice, grievance contact, and data-retention process.
  • Provider and data-processing review for hosting, storage, email, and payment services.
  • The governing law, complaint handling, and dispute resolution steps.

Until these items are completed, all pricing, admissions, and payment details remain provisional and no live contract is created by submitting the website form.
