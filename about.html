About the academy

Technology education built around practical progress.

Y-Hat Analytics is an online technology training institute helping learners and working professionals build capability in programming, data, cloud, and AI through structured learning and applied practice.

Our purpose

Make complex skills easier to learn and apply.

Our purpose is to make technology education clear, relevant, and grounded in practice. Learning paths span programming, analytics, cloud, data engineering, AI, technical communication, and professional skills—helping learners connect concepts to the work they want to do.

Who we serve

For learners at different stages of their journey.

Our online format is designed for people building a first foundation, moving toward a technology role, or extending skills they use at work. We value a collaborative classroom where learners can ask questions, compare approaches, and learn from different perspectives.

Online-first by design

Our current model is online training. Before each cohort, we will confirm the teaching schedule, time zone, meeting and learning tools, support arrangements, recordings policy, and any technical requirements in writing.

Honest career guidance

Where offered, career preparation may include project presentation, CV feedback, or interview practice. These are learning and guidance services—not a guarantee of employment, placement, certification, or a particular outcome.
